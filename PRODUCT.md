# PennyWise

PennyWise is a local personal finance workspace for manual income, expenses, transfers, separate accounts, category budgets, bills, and savings reservations. It works on phones and desktops and reopens offline after the production app caches its files. There is no signup or cloud recovery.

The Daily Companion overview puts balances, income, expenses, remaining budget, and a safe-to-spend estimate first. Weekly spending, category breakdowns, budget health, unpaid bills, and searchable activity explain the figures without invented trends or sample balances.

Quick entry supports the N shortcut outside editable fields, category choices, a remembered active account, and guarded dismissal of unsaved input. Bills create linked expenses when marked paid. Recording a payment never sends money to a provider.

Savings goals reserve existing money. Contributions and releases do not create income, expenses, or bank transfers. A funded goal cannot be deleted until its funds are released. Later spending can leave reservations underfunded; the app warns rather than rewriting history.

Reports filter by an inclusive date range, type, and category. CSV export contains matching records. The PDF action opens a printable report and the browser print dialog, where the user chooses Save as PDF.

The local profile stores a name, light/dark/system theme, alert preference, and one ledger denomination, PHP or USD. Currency cannot change while monetary records exist. There is no conversion or multi-currency wallet.

Version-two backups include savings and preferences. Imports combine records after a preview and explicit conflict choices. Version-one PHP backups remain readable and preserve newer local records and settings. Browser storage can be lost, so users need private export backups.

Cloud sync, shared finances, bank connections, push alerts, debt planning, and tax guidance remain outside this release.
